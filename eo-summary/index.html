AI/ML Earth Observation:
Use Cases for Sustainability & Business Intelligence

PublishedMay 2026
Categories13 sectors
ScopeDirect & indirect EO applications
FocusPositive environmental & social outcomes

A synthesis of how AI/ML-analyzed satellite data is being deployed across industries — both as a direct operational tool and as the underlying evidence base for environmental regulatory compliance, sustainability disclosure, and nature finance.

Earth observation (EO) — satellite imagery and remote sensing data analyzed by artificial intelligence — has matured from a defense and research technology into commercial infrastructure for environmental accountability. Across more than a dozen industry sectors, businesses are using AI-processed satellite data to make operational decisions, verify environmental commitments, meet rapidly expanding regulatory disclosure requirements and assess climate risks.

This summary categorizes key commercial use cases by sector, with emphasis on EXISTING platforms/services on the market that drive genuine environmental and social outcomes. EO is increasingly recognized as the only mechanism capable of providing continuous, scalable, and auditable environmental assessment and monitoring at the scale these obligations demand.

Key Regulatory Frameworks Driving EO Adoption

Mandatory and voluntary disclosure requirements creating institutional demand for satellite-grade environmental data
CSRD (EU)
Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive — mandatory for ~50,000 EU companies (phased 2024–2026). Requires asset-level physical climate risk and nature impact data across ESRS E1–E5.
TCFD
Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures — mandatory in UK, NZ, Singapore, and others. Physical risk assessment requires location-specific hazard data that EO provides.
TNFD
Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures — voluntary but rapidly adopted. LEAP framework requires location-specific biodiversity and ecosystem condition data — EO's core output.
EUDR
EU Deforestation Regulation — mandatory Dec 2025 (large co.) / June 2026 (SMEs). Requires satellite-verifiable deforestation-free due diligence for 7 commodity groups.
ISSB S1 / S2
International Sustainability Standards Board — global baseline sustainability disclosure standards being adopted across major economies. Builds on TCFD; physical risk data requirements are identical.
EU Methane Regulation
Mandatory monitoring and reporting of methane emissions for oil, gas, and coal operators in the EU — independent satellite verification increasingly used to validate self-reports.
GBF / Kunming-Montreal
Global Biodiversity Framework — 196 nations committed to protect 30% of land and sea by 2030 (30x30). Nature finance instruments and TNFD disclosures operationalize private sector contributions.
Biodiversity Net Gain (UK)
Mandatory since Feb 2024 — all English planning permissions require a 10% biodiversity increase, verified via standardized habitat metrics that EO-based mapping can support at scale.
